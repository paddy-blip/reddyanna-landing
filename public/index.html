Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

What is a Cricket ID?

A Cricket ID is a personal online account that lets you follow live matches, view markets and play on supported sports platforms. Reddy Anna creates and activates the ID for you and shares the login details privately on WhatsApp.

How quickly will my ID be ready?

Most IDs are activated within a few minutes of completing the short verification and first deposit. During very busy match days it can take slightly longer, and our team keeps you updated on WhatsApp.

What do I need to get started?

Just your name and a WhatsApp number. You must be 18 years or older. Our team will guide you through the remaining steps in the chat.

Which payment methods are supported?

We support popular Indian payment methods such as UPI and bank transfer. The exact options available are shared by our team when you request your ID.

How do withdrawals work?

Request a withdrawal from your account or through our support team. Withdrawals are processed to your verified account, usually the same day.

Is my personal information safe?

Yes. We only ask for the details needed to create your ID, never share them with third parties for marketing, and send login credentials only to your registered WhatsApp number.

Is online betting legal where I live?

Laws on online gaming and betting differ between Indian states and countries. It is your responsibility to check and follow the laws that apply where you live before creating an ID. This website does not provide legal advice.

Can I get help at any time?

Yes. Our support team is available 24/7 on WhatsApp for ID requests, deposits, withdrawals and any account questions.
