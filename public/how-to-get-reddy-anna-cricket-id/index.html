How to Get Your Reddy Anna Cricket ID in 5 Simple Steps

2 October 2026 2 min read

Getting an online Cricket ID should take minutes, not hours. At Reddy Anna the whole process runs through a single WhatsApp conversation, so there are no forms to download and no long waits. Here is exactly what to expect.

What you need before you start

  • A mobile number with WhatsApp
  • Your full name
  • A UPI app or bank account for your first deposit
  • You must be 18 years or older

Step 1: Message our team on WhatsApp

Tap any “Get Your ID” button on this website. It opens a WhatsApp chat with a short pre-written message, so all you need to do is press send. A member of our support team replies within minutes.

Step 2: Share your basic details

Tell us your name and which sport you mainly follow — cricket, IPL, football or tennis. That helps us set up the right ID for you. We never ask for passwords, OTPs or card PINs.

Step 3: Make your first deposit

Our team shares the official payment details for your deposit. Use only the details sent in your chat, and keep a screenshot of your payment for reference.

Step 4: Receive your activated ID

Once your deposit is confirmed, your Cricket ID and login details are sent privately to your WhatsApp. In most cases this happens within about five minutes.

Step 5: Log in and change your password

Log in on your phone, tablet or laptop and change your password straight away. Then you are ready to follow live matches.

Tips to keep your account secure

  • Never share your login details with anyone — including people claiming to be from support.
  • Only pay to details sent by our official WhatsApp number.
  • Use a strong password you don’t use anywhere else.
  • Set a personal budget before you play. Read our Responsible Gaming guidelines.

Have questions? See our FAQ or contact our team — we are available 24/7.
